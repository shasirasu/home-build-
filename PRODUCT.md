# Home Build 3D

## What it is
A website where a person gives the 2D architecture plan of a house and gets it back as a 3D model: built from the drawing like a factory line (plan data in, house out), viewable from outside, walkable inside, with the detailed structure exposed, items to get, and the house designable (furniture, finishes, colours).

## Who uses it
Home owners, families and small builders in India planning a G+1 / G+2 house from an architect's or draughtsman's plan (PDF or image, dimensioned in feet and inches). They want to understand the house before it is built, show it to family, and know what it takes to build and furnish.

## What it must prove
- The 3D house is the plan: rooms, sizes, doors and stairs line up with the drawing (the plan stays visible as an underlay to check).
- You can walk in through the main door, up the stairs and into every room.
- The structure is visible: columns, beams, slabs, footings, walls.
- Items: furniture and fixtures to place, plus the quantities and indicative costs to build and furnish.
- The outside can be designed: a modern style to start from, a finish for every surface (including the owner's own picture), modern details, and the outdoor lights, seen at night.

## Truths and limits
- The site builds the house itself. A CAD-exported PDF is read from its own lines and text in the browser. When it cannot read a plan (photo, scan), Google Gemini is used only to understand the drawing; the build stays in the site, and the result is shown as a draft to check.
- Heights, windows and structure sizes are rarely on a 2D plan; anything assumed is marked assumed and editable.
- The outside finish is never on a plan: it is the owner's design, starting from a modern default.
- Costs are indicative estimates with editable rates, never quotes.
- First sample: "House Plan-Model.pdf", a 22' × 36' G+1 house.

## Surfaces
- Intro (persuade): perspective tunnel hero, supplied by the client.
- Studio (operate): 3D viewer, walk mode, design, items and quantities.
- Plan (operate): 2D plan editor over the uploaded drawing.
